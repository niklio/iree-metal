# Governance

iree-metal is currently a maintainer-led experimental project. Nik Liolios
(`@niklio`) is the release manager and final reviewer for fork-specific code,
submodule pins, support claims, and release publication.

Technical decisions are made in public issues and pull requests. Decisions
prioritize correctness, reproducibility, transparent measurement, upstream
compatibility, and a small support surface. Additional maintainers can be added
after sustained, high-quality contributions and demonstrated stewardship of
reviews and releases.

The upstream IREE project retains its own governance. Nothing in this document
grants authority to speak for IREE, OpenXLA, Apple, or their maintainers.
